Export Service Process

1. Inquiry & Sourcing: Receiving buyer requirements (OE numbers, target price, quantities) and matching with optimal suppliers.


2. Quotation & Negotiation: Providing competitive pricing and negotiating trade terms (EXW, FOB, CIF, etc.).


3. Quality Inspection: Conducting strict product specification matching and quality checks prior to packing.


4. Export Packing & Logistics: Providing export standard packaging (Pallets, Wooden Boxes) and handling shipping documents (CI, PL, B/L).
